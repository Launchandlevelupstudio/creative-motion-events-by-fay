What is Events by Fay?

Beautiful spaces.
Real experiences.

From Creative Motion Events comes something new. Events by Fay is a collection of curated experiences hosted throughout the DMV, created for people who want more than somewhere to go.

Every event will be different: business, celebration, wellness, culture, creativity, food, and simply having a good time. What they share is intentional design, good energy, and connection.
