Private Celebrations

For the moments that took decades to arrive.

Tailored, luxurious experiences for your most special life moments: milestone birthdays, anniversaries, intimate dinners and destination celebrations, designed with elegance and produced with calm.

Celebrations we design

Milestone birthdays

A fortieth, a fiftieth, or the birthday that took years to arrive. We design the room around the person being celebrated, so they can simply be present.

Anniversaries

An anniversary, designed as its own evening. Mood, color, tablescape and flow are shaped for the couple, then produced with the same calm we bring to every room.

Engagement dinners

An engagement dinner, designed as an intimate celebration. Tablescape, florals and place settings, directed so the evening feels elegant and calm.

Intimate dinners

Intimate dinners and small gatherings, scaled with the same elegance. The table, the florals and the pace of the room are designed so hosts can be present with their guests.

Destination celebrations

Destination celebrations, taken by invitation. The same concept, vendor direction, install and breakdown, for a milestone being marked away from home.

Ways to work together

Fayola designs the room and directs the production herself, so you have one point of contact from first conversation to breakdown.

Design

Event design & creative direction

A full creative concept: mood, color, texture and flow, with tablescape and installation design. A proposal follows in five business days, and the design fee is shown separately from rentals and florals.

Production

Full-service production

Full vendor sourcing and direction, florals and décor sourcing, custom signage and printed material, and budget oversight and timeline management. Then install lead, on-site direction and breakdown, with photography and videography coordination.

Sourcing covers linens, florals, custom pieces, signage and rentals. Venue and catering are separate.
